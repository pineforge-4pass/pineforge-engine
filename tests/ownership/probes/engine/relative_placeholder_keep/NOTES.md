# relative_placeholder_keep

D6 row 16 KEEP control for a finite negative relative target. A pending short parent fills at 100 with profit=200 and mintick=1. Its raw target is -100, but source_trigger_threshold deliberately projects a non-positive level to zero. With calc_on_order_fills off, resolve_anchored_level therefore arms a Limit at zero; it does not enter the invalid-installed-level tick-placeholder branch. The fill-point re-run cannot adopt this child and withdraws it in that notification. The explicit later close completes with a trade. The C ABI source witness pins the zero trigger, the same child's Armed/Cancelled identity and ordinal order, and every trade money field against a no-exit twin. A separate C++ control uses the existing raw-limit policy fixture to exercise the actual tick=1 placeholder branch; this Pine probe does not claim that branch coverage.

Authoring only. Expectations were pinned before execution. Feed rule: 40 one-minute bars starting at 1700000040000 ms, O=H=L=C at the prices described above, volume=1. Each next timestamp is exactly 60000 ms later. Data is synthetic and independent of market/private fixtures. run.json uses P0's options schema; syminfo fixes tick/point value/quantity step to 1 and a UTC 24x7 calendar.
